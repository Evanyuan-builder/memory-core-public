## Pacing notes for AI TTS (剪映)

- Voice preset: pick a clear, slightly warm American or British male voice (e.g. 剪映 → 文本朗读 → "解说小帅" / "解说雅典娜" English equivalent, or upload to ElevenLabs `Adam`/`Rachel` if available).
- Speed: 1.0×. Don't speed up — judges are non-native speakers and the on-screen visuals need room to land.
- Pauses: 0.4 s between sentences, 0.8 s between sections.
- Pronunciation tweaks if AI mangles them:
  - "Evanyuan" → ee-van-yoo-an (or just say "Evan" in CTA if the long handle sounds clunky)
  - "LongMemEval-S" → "long mem eval ess"
  - "LoCoMo" → "lo co mo"
  - "MIT" → letters M-I-T

## Subtitle style

- Burnt-in, English. Sans-serif, white text, soft black outline at 60% opacity.
- One line at a time, max ~10 words per line.
- Position: bottom center, ~12% from bottom edge.
